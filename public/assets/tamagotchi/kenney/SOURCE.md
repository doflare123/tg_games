# Kenney Furniture Kit 2.0

- Author: Kenney, https://kenney.nl/
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Downloaded: 2026-11-15, from the official download link on the source page.
- License: CC0 1.0; the original license is included as `LICENSE.txt`.
- Files: selected, unmodified PNGs from the archive's `Isometric/` directory.
- Sprites retain their original camera and lighting. The cabinet, radio and
  tabletop plant use the SW orientation to align with the room's right wall;
  other furniture uses SE. Small accessories and table lamps select SE or SW
  to match the piece of furniture supporting them. The table-lamp variants are
  `lampRoundTable_SE.png` and `lampRoundTable_SW.png`; books also use `books_SW.png`.

These sprites are used in the Tamagotchi living-room prototype. Starter furniture
can be moved or stored separately from the paid catalogue and grants no score.
