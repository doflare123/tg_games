# Toy pet sprites, version 1

Created for this project on 2026-11-15 with the built-in `image_gen` tool.
The PNG retains its original transparent alpha channel; it is not a Kenney asset.
Layout: three columns by two rows, 512 × 512 pixels per cell. Top: cat, fox,
rabbit. Bottom: panda, dog, axolotl. CSS selects a cell without modifying the PNG.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game SPRITE SHEET, exactly THREE COLUMNS and TWO ROWS, a single rectangular 3:2 sheet divided into six equal SQUARE invisible cells on genuine TRANSPARENT alpha background. No visible grid, no letters, no text. Each cell contains exactly ONE full-body cute animal sprite, centered with equal 15% transparent margins, all feet on the same relative baseline. Top row, left to right: cream tabby CAT with short triangular ears and curved tail; orange FOX with large pointed dark ears and fluffy white-tipped tail; cream RABBIT with tall pink-lined ears. Bottom row, left to right: black-and-cream PANDA with round ears and eye patches; tan floppy-eared PUPPY; pale pink AXOLOTL with six pink external gill branches and little legs. Style: cohesive soft low-poly 3D toy game assets, simple faceted clay-like matte surfaces, clean geometric volumes, restrained warm cream/terracotta/sage palette, tiny dark friendly eyes, small muzzles, subtle expression, no black outlines, no glossy plastic, no fuzzy fur, no clothing, no accessories. The six sprites MUST look like the same production set. Camera: identical fixed orthographic isometric view elevated about 35 degrees, animals seated in three-quarter view facing toward lower-left/front of the screen; visible top of heads and backs. Lighting: identical warm soft light from upper-left, gentle darkening toward lower-right. Keep all body parts including tail and ears entirely within their own cell with generous transparent margins. Equal apparent body scale, rabbit ears may be taller. Individual animals only, NO room, NO furniture, NO floor, NO ground platform, NO backdrop, NO checkerboard. A very faint compact contact shadow immediately under each set of feet is acceptable, no large cast shadows. Output should be a crisp useful sprite sheet with clearly separated silhouettes and exact uniform cell layout, not a collage or illustration.
